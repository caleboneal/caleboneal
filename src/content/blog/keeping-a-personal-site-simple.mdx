---
title: "Notes on keeping a personal site simple"
description: "Why a thin layout, a small content collection, and an RSS feed go further than another redesign."
pubDate: 2026-07-12
---

Personal sites get complicated fast: frameworks, themes, social widgets, analytics, and half-finished redesigns.

The useful version is smaller. A name people can find, a few links, a place to publish writing, and a feed for anyone who still wants it. Everything else can wait.
